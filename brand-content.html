Planning your project.

Can you use our existing brand identity?

Yes. We work with your brand guidelines and existing assets to keep Facebook, Instagram and ad creative consistent. A full rebrand or logo redesign is outside the core content scope.

What content can you create?

Facebook and Instagram posts, captions, short-form video, Meta ad creative and assets for Google Display or YouTube can be scoped. Your proposal defines formats, quantities and production requirements.

Do you help with ad copy and captions?

Yes. We can develop campaign messages, social captions and ad copy around your offer, audience and existing tone of voice.

Will we receive editable files?

File formats and editable deliverables are agreed before the project begins.

Can you direct a photo shoot?

Creative direction and shot planning can be included for campaign and social content. Photography, filming, talent and other production costs are scoped separately.

How many concepts do we review?

The number of creative routes and review rounds is set out clearly in your proposal.

Where does the project begin?

We review your offer, brand guidelines, Facebook and Instagram presence, and Google Ads or Meta Ads creative needs before agreeing the content brief.

How do we give feedback?

We use agreed review stages and a consolidated feedback process to keep decisions clear.

Can you manage Facebook and Instagram posting?

Yes. A content calendar, publishing and community management can be added to the scope. We agree post volumes, approval dates, response coverage and account permissions first.

Can you support a launch?

Yes. We can prepare the social content, ad copy, images and video for an agreed Google Ads or Meta Ads launch. Paid campaign setup and management are separate services.

Are assets prepared for each platform?

We prepare approved assets for the agreed Google, Facebook and Instagram placements, checking the required formats before handover.
