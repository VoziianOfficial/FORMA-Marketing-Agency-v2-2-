A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

✳
Where do we start?+

Share your website, offer, target market and budget. Tell us whether you need Google Ads, Meta Ads or content support. We’ll review the enquiry and discuss account access and the right starting scope.

Can we work on just one service?+

Yes. Start with Google Ads, Meta Ads or Social Media & Content. We can also plan Google and Meta campaigns together. Platforms, campaign types and deliverables are agreed in your proposal.

Is advertising spend included?+

No. Google and Meta media spend is paid separately from our management and creative fees. Setup work, production and any additional tools are scoped before work begins.

How do you measure success?+

We agree relevant conversions, such as qualified enquiries or purchases, and review spend and available performance data. Google Analytics 4 and platform reporting provide context; attribution can differ between tools. Specific results are not guaranteed.

Do you support tracking setup?+

We can review Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads conversion tracking and Meta Pixel requirements. Conversions API and other implementation work are scoped around your website, technical resources and applicable consent requirements.
